A Practical Preparation Routine

Set aside a manageable daily study period and keep an error notebook. Start with a short diagnostic exercise to identify weak areas, then spend more time on those topics.

A sample study session could include 20 minutes of English, 20 minutes of mathematics, and 20 minutes of general awareness or reasoning. Review incorrect answers instead of repeatedly attempting questions you already know.

Plan physical preparation separately so fatigue does not undermine either activity. In the final days, check travel arrangements, required documents, and reporting time. Do not use the last evening for an unfamiliar intense workout or an all-night study session.
