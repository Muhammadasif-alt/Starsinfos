FPSC ki General Recruitment job ke liye teen kaam zaroori hain: sahi post select karke 17-digit PSID banayein, us PSID par fee jama karein, phir portal par wapas aa kar Step-2 submit karein. Bank ka receipt milna fee ki tasdeeq hai. Application submit hone ki pehchan Tracking ID aur application printout hai. Agar aap ki screen par pehle account banane ka option aaye to apne CNIC se register karein. Agar General Recruitment ki screen seedha post aur CNIC maange, wahin se shuru karein.

Yeh guide 3 November 2026 ko FPSC ki published instructions dekh kar tayyar ki gayi hai. Neeche di gayi fee aur correction ka rule General Recruitment (GR) ke liye hai. CSS competitive examination ke liye usi saal aur exam stage ka alag notice parhein.

1. FPSC latest jobs aur advertisement mein apna case number dhoondein

FPSC ki official website kholein, General Recruitment ki advertisements dekhein aur jis post mein dilchaspi ho us ka case number, BS grade, department aur closing date likh lein. Ek hi advertisement mein kai departments ke liye milte-julte naam ho sakte hain. Misal ke taur par, Assistant Director ka title dekh kar fee jama na karein jab tak department aur qualification dono match na karein.

Sirf PDF ki upload date ko application deadline na samjhein.

2. Fee dene se pehle eligibility aur documents check karein

FPSC ki General Recruitment instructions ke mutabiq age, qualification aur experience advertisement ki closing date tak dekhe jaate hain. Apni date of birth, degree ka subject, required experience aur domicile ko apni post ki entry se milayein. Male aur female candidates ke liye applicable quota bhi usi entry mein dekhein. Sirf graduate hona har BS-16 ya BS-17 post ke liye eligibility nahin banata.

Lecturer ke liye subject ka naam, Inspector ke liye agar diye gaye hon to physical standards, aur Medical Officer ke liye advertisement mein likhi professional registration khaas taur par check karein. Yeh mukhtalif posts ko parhne ki misaalein hain; inhein current vacancies ki list na samjhein.

3. FPSC online registration: account banayein ya CNIC se shuru karein

Official website ka Apply Online option use karein. Agar aap FPSC Applicant Portal par hain aur pehle account nahin banaya, apne CNIC ke saath account banane ka option chun kar screen par maangi gayi details dein. Pehle se account hai to usi se sign in karein. Aisa email aur mobile number dein jo test aur interview ke dauran bhi aap ke paas rahe.

4. Step-1: FPSC online application form aur challan

Post select karein, CNIC darj karein aur eligibility tasdeeq karne ke baad basic details bharein. Domicile ka district certificate se dekhein. Test centre aur interview centre ke fields alag hon to dono parhein. Transcript ki issue date maangi jaye to document par likhi date dein; andaaze se graduation ceremony ki date na likhein.

Details check karke Generate PSID & Challan select karein. Jo 17-digit PSID mile, us ka printout ya PDF save karein. “FPSC challan form 2026 download” dhoondhne walon ke liye zaroori baat: current GR payment aap ke liye portal se generate hue PSID par hoti hai. Kisi dost ka challan, internet se mila blank TR-6 ya purana treasury form is ka badal nahin hai.

Mobile banking ki misaalon mein Easypaisa aur JazzCash bhi diye gaye hain. App mein bill payment ka relevant option use karein; kisi shakhs ke wallet ya account mein aam money transfer na karein.

Misal ke taur par, agar aap BS-17 ki post ke liye apply kar rahe hain, apna generated PSID enter karein aur payment confirm karne se pehle Rs. 600 aur bill details check karein. Amount ya reference match na kare to transaction rok kar challan dobara dekhein. Payment ke baad receipt, transaction reference aur tareekh save karein. Do posts ke liye apply karte waqt har application ke liye usi ka generated PSID istemal karein.

6. Step-2: final submission aur status check

Payment ke baad General Recruitment → Apply Online par wapas aayein. Usi post aur CNIC ke saath baqi form complete karein. Qualification aur experience ki dates preview mein dobara dekhein, phir final submission karein. Tracking ID aur application ki copy save karein. File ka naam case number ke saath rakhein, khaas taur par agar aap ek se zyada posts ke candidate hain.

Official GR system — Step-2, printout, Tracking ID aur correction ke liye:

Apply Now

Agar paisay kat gaye magar final form nahin khul raha, bank reference ko Tracking ID na samjhein. Pehle transaction ka status, CNIC, case aur PSID milayein. Masla baqi rahe to FPSC ki technical helpline 051-111-000-248 par office hours mein rabta karein. Dobara payment se pehle pehli transaction confirm karna behtar hai.

Correction: FPSC ki GR instructions ke mutabiq final submission ke baad closing date tak sirf ek martaba application edit ki ja sakti hai. Is liye pehli typo dekhte hi edit save karne ke bajaye poora form parhein, phir saari zaroori durustiyan ek saath karein. Closing ke baad naya experience claim karne par bharosa na karein.

7. Roll number slip, written test syllabus aur interview

Tracking ID form ka reference hai; exam hall ke liye Online Admission Certificate, jise roll number slip bhi kehte hain, alag hota hai. FPSC website par apne GR phase aur case ka notice dekhein. Slip milne par naam, venue, reporting time aur saath lane wali cheezen parhein. Written test syllabus bhi case number se match karein: har Assistant Director ya Inspector post ke liye ek hi paper assume na karein.

Past papers PDF ko practice ke liye use karein aur pehle subject, post aur saal check karein. Preparation ka behtar aghaz official syllabus se hai: har topic ke saamne apni kamzor cheez likhein, phir timed questions solve karein. Written result aur interview schedule alag notices ho sakte hain. Agar document submission ki deadline di gayi ho to interview date ka intezar na karein.

Bottom line

Apne paas teen records rakhein: case number batata hai kis post ke liye apply kiya, PSID aur receipt batate hain fee kis application ke liye di, aur Tracking ID ke saath final printout batata hai form submit hua. Fee jama karne ke baad Step-2 zaroor poora karein.
