What Is an ATS and Why Does It Matter in Pakistan?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software employers use to collect, organize, and search job applications. It reads (parses) your CV, stores your details in fields such as name, job titles, and skills, and lets recruiters search by keyword.

An ATS is mainly built to organize applicants for recruiters, not to make final hiring decisions. But if it cannot read your CV correctly, your details may be incomplete or jumbled, so a recruiter may never see your strengths.

Pakistani banks, telecom companies, large firms, and multinationals increasingly use ATS tools, according to one CV-tool provider, and job portals such as Rozee.pk match applicants using profile keywords.

How Do You Format a CV So an ATS Can Read It? (Step by Step)

  1. Use a single-column layout. Many systems read line by line from left to right, so columns can scramble the order.
  2. Use standard section headings. Stick to Work Experience, Education, Skills, and Certifications. ATS tools are trained to recognize these labels.
  3. Choose a simple, common font such as Arial or Calibri, in a readable size.
  4. Skip tables, text boxes, and graphics. Parsers may produce garbled text from tables or skip text boxes entirely.
  5. Do not put key information in the header or footer. Most systems cannot read them, so keep your name and phone number in the main body.
  6. Use plain bullet points to list duties and achievements.
  7. Save in the right file type. See the next section.

Should You Upload a PDF or Word (.docx) CV?

Follow the job advertisement first. If it names a file type, use that.

  • A text-based PDF or a .docx file is generally the safest choice.
  • Never upload a scanned image or a photo of your CV, because the ATS cannot read text inside images.
  • Some career centres say some systems struggle with PDFs and prefer .docx. Others say PDF is fine if it is text-based. If unsure, keep both versions.

Test your PDF by opening it and trying to select and copy the text. If you cannot, it is probably an image.

How Do You Use Keywords From the Job Advertisement?

Recruiters search ATS databases with keywords, so your CV needs the same terms the employer uses.

  1. Read the job advertisement and underline job titles, tools, skills, and qualifications.
  2. Match their wording where it is true for you. If they write "Microsoft Excel," do not only write "spreadsheets."
  3. Place keywords naturally in your summary, skills, and experience bullets.
  4. Do not keyword-stuff or hide white text. Humans read your CV later, and false claims can hurt you at the interview stage.
  5. Write both the full term and the short form once, for example "Search Engine Optimization (SEO)."

What Should You Remove From a Pakistani CV?

Many older local CVs include a long list of personal details. For private-sector online applications, modern recruiters generally do not need:

  • Photograph: ATS cannot read it, and one tool provider warns a photo at the top can push your name and contact details out of the area a parser reads
  • CNIC number
  • Father's or husband's name
  • Religion and marital status
  • Full home address (city and country are enough)
  • Date of birth, unless requested

Important exception: Pakistani government and public-sector application forms often do ask for father's name, CNIC, date of birth, domicile, and a photograph. In that case, put these details on the official form, not necessarily in your CV, and follow the advertisement exactly.

How Do You Write Experience Bullets That Work for ATS and Humans?

Start each bullet with a strong verb, add what you did, and include a result.

  • Weak: "Responsible for sales."
  • Better: "Increased monthly sales by 15% by following up with 40 clients each week."

Use real numbers only.

Use consistent date formats, such as "Jan 2023 - Mar 2025," because parsers read dates to calculate your experience.

What Length and Language Should a Pakistani CV Be?

  • One to two pages is the norm. Fresh graduates should aim for one.
  • Write in clear, professional English, unless the advertisement asks for another language.
  • Use past tense for old jobs and present tense for your current job.

What Are Common ATS CV Mistakes to Avoid?

  1. Using a fancy template with columns and icons
  2. Uploading a scanned or image-based file
  3. Putting contact details in the header
  4. Using creative section titles
  5. Skipping keywords from the advertisement
  6. Sending the same CV to every job without changes
  7. Using skill bars or star ratings instead of words
  8. Misspelled names, job titles, or tools

Be careful with templates labelled "ATS-friendly." Some still contain columns or graphics, so test them.

How Can You Test Your CV?

  1. Copy and paste your CV into a plain text editor. If the order is jumbled or text is missing, a parser may struggle too.
  2. Open the PDF and try to select text.
  3. Ask a friend to find your name, phone, and last job title in 10 seconds.
  4. Compare your CV to the job advertisement and tick off each key requirement.
