Under the PM Youth Chromebook Scheme 2026, there is no separate list of "selected schools." Students are selected individually on merit: those who score at least 50% in their latest Grade 9 (SSC-I) exam are ranked within their province or region, education board, and academic stream until the quota is filled. The Inter Boards Coordination Commission (IBCC) verifies results, and the student's school then confirms enrolment and affiliation before a Chromebook is couriered.

What Is the PM Youth Chromebook Scheme 2026?

It is a federal programme to distribute 500,000 refurbished Chromebooks to high-performing Grade 9 students. The Ministry of Federal Education and Professional Training (MoFEPT) runs it with the Prime Minister's Youth Programme, and the TT Care Foundation has committed to supply the devices under a letter of understanding.

Officials describe it as a scheme that rewards results rather than recommendations. Reports differ on the rollout period (two years in some reports, three years in others), so treat the timeline as unconfirmed.

How Are Schools Selected for the PM Youth Chromebook Scheme?

They are not selected in advance. We found no official "PM Youth Chromebook Scheme selected schools list" and no "district wise school list." Instead, schools play a verification role:

  1. Your education board verifies your SSC-I result.
  2. The IBCC validates results and helps prepare merit lists.
  3. Provincial education departments and education foundations confirm enrolment and school affiliation.
  4. Before dispatch, your school verifies your mark sheet, current student registration, and current school affiliation.

Be careful with any website claiming to publish a "selected schools list" for money or for clicks.

PM Youth Chromebook Scheme Eligibility Criteria and Student Selection Criteria

  • Class requirements: Grade 9 (SSC-I) students in Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and Balochistan. One guide (result.pk) says Islamabad, AJK, and Gilgit-Baltistan also include Grade 10 (SSC-II); confirm this with the IBCC.
  • Academic performance requirements: minimum 50% in the latest SSC-I exam.
  • Merit, not just marks: meeting 50% does not guarantee a device. Selection depends on merit within your province or region, board, and stream.
  • Exam year: based on the 2026 annual examinations.
  • Madaris: registered madaris students are also eligible and are vetted separately through the Directorate General of Religious Education. The Prime Minister also said seminary students are eligible.

Punjab has the largest quota. Eligible Punjab students are high-scoring Grade 9 students with at least 50% in SSC-I, ranked within their education board and stream.

KP students follow the same SSC-I merit rule, ranked within their board and stream against KP's quota.

PM Youth Chromebook Scheme Apply Online 2026: Application Process

Reports describe these steps (this is the student registration 2026 process):

  1. Visit the official portal: pmychromebooks.ibcc.edu.pk.
  2. Select your education board.
  3. Enter your B-Form number or roll number to search your record.
  4. Verify the details shown, such as your name, school, and marks.
  5. Upload the required documents.
  6. Submit and save your reference slip (a tracking code) to follow your application.

If your record is not found, contact your education board or school.

PM Youth Chromebook Scheme Required Documents

Guides report that you need:

  • A recent photograph
  • Your SSC-I mark sheet
  • B-Form or CNIC copy (one guide says attested)
  • Father's or guardian's CNIC copy (one guide says attested)

One guide also says each file must be under 300 KB. These details come from third-party guides, so confirm them on the portal.

What Is the School Registration Process and Verification Process?

Schools do not apply as institutions. The PM Youth Chromebook Scheme school verification process works like this: after the merit list is final, each device is linked to a recipient by serial number and checked against merit, enrolment, institutional affiliation, and delivery details. Your school and education foundation or department confirm enrolment, then the device goes out.

What Is the Merit List for 2026 and When Is It Published?

The PM Youth Chromebook Scheme merit list 2026 is prepared by examination boards and the IBCC. Reports say no confirmed nationwide merit list publication date has been announced. One notice says only shortlisted students' names are sent to the IBCC by the respective boards. Check your board's website for your list.

PM Youth Chromebook Scheme Distribution Process and Chromebook Distribution Date

Laptops will go directly to verified students by courier, with a central monitoring system tracking dispatch, delivery, failed attempts, and receipt confirmation. Repairs and maintenance will be handled by a contracted firm after the first year of delivery.

Distribution date: no confirmed date has been announced in the sources we reviewed. Do not trust a social media post that gives a precise date without an official link.

How to Check PM Youth Chromebook Scheme School Selection Status

  1. Log in or search your record on pmychromebooks.ibcc.edu.pk using your board and B-Form or roll number.
  2. Use your reference slip or tracking code to follow verification and dispatch.
  3. Watch your education board's website for merit lists and deadlines.
  4. If something is wrong, use the grievance committees. Officials say these will exist at examination board and provincial or regional levels for complaints on merit ranking, list inclusion or exclusion, enrolment checks, exam data, and devices not received.

What Is the Last Date to Apply?

There is no single nationwide deadline. The deadline varies by education board, so check your board's notice and the portal.

PM Youth Chromebook Scheme for Parents: What to Do Now

  • Keep your child's SSC-I mark sheet, B-Form, and CNICs ready.
  • Check that the name and roll number match across school and board records. Mismatches can affect verification.

  • Do not enter details on links shared in WhatsApp groups or comments.
