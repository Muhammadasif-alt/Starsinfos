Eligibility: age, domicile and monthly salary

The advertised basic age range is reported as 18–25 years, with 15 November 2026 used as the age cut-off. Calculate your age on that date rather than on test day. If you depend on age relaxation, check the wording that applies to your post and circumstances before paying. An age concession does not change the physical measurements.

Women are eligible to apply. A candidate with Punjab domicile should check the Punjab allocation for the chosen rank. The presence of Punjab seats for constables does not establish the same allocation for the two SI posts. Enter the domicile recorded on your certificate; the city where you currently live or train may be different.

The notice gives pay scales rather than a fixed Dolphin Force salary per month. BS-07 is the constable grade, not a rupee figure. Running test preparation: learn your four lap times

Use a measured track if available. Four laps equal 1.6 km only on a 400-metre circuit; “four rounds of the park” may be a very different distance. Use a stopwatch that shows elapsed time. The table gives cumulative checkpoints, so you do not need to restart the watch at each lap.

Use the checkpoints to identify the actual pacing problem. A runner targeting seven minutes who reaches 400 metres in 1:30 is already 15 seconds ahead of an even pace. If their final lap consistently takes more than two minutes, practising a controlled first lap may be more useful than trying to sprint the first 100 metres faster.

If you are new to running, begin with comfortable walking and short jogs on a measured route. Record how far you can cover comfortably before attempting timed practice. The application deadline is not your physical test date, and completing a hard run every day is not a condition of applying. The advertisement coverage advises a medical check before the endurance test. If you are unwell or have concerns about strenuous exercise, get individual advice before attempting a timed run.

If you already run regularly, practise recognizing your target pace over a single measured lap, recover, and note whether you can repeat the pace without a fast start. On a later practice day, record the halfway split over a longer effort. Keep the shoes and timing method familiar. On test day, follow the officials’ distance markings, wear suitable running shoes and take drinking water as directed.
