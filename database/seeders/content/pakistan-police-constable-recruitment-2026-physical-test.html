There is no single running test for police constables across Pakistan. In the November 2026 Islamabad Dolphin Force recruitment, men are asked to run 1.6 km in seven minutes and women 1.6 km in ten minutes. A Sindh Police district constable advertisement, by contrast, specifies an 800-metre walk in 14 minutes for women. Before you pay a fee or start timing laps, identify your force, district, post and recruitment batch. Those four details determine which physical test, medical standards and application deadline apply to you.

Age, matric qualification and required documents

There is no nationwide police constable age limit. The Islamabad Dolphin intake reports a basic range of 18–25, while the September Balochistan advertisement listed 18–28 for men and 18–30 for women. Calculate your age on the cut-off date in the notice. If you need an age concession, verify that it covers your category; it does not automatically change a height or endurance requirement.

For matric-based constable jobs, have your certificate or mark sheet, CNIC, recent photograph and domicile ready. Sindh applicants should also check whether a permanent residence certificate, such as PRC Form D, is required. Driver applicants should read the licence category and validity requirements. Compare your name, date of birth and district across the documents before submitting. Keep scans for registration and originals for the stage named in your call letter.

Running test preparation: turn your target into lap times

On a measured 400-metre track, 1.6 km is four laps. For a seven-minute run, the checkpoints are 1:45, 3:30, 5:15 and 7:00. An eight-minute run allows two minutes per lap; a ten-minute run allows two minutes and thirty seconds. For the 800-metre walk above, a steady pace would put you at 400 metres in seven minutes. Practise the movement specified in your notice.

Use a stopwatch that shows total elapsed time. If your target is eight minutes and your first lap takes 1:40 but your second takes 2:20, you are on target at halfway, yet your pace has varied sharply. On another practice day, try a more even first lap and compare how you finish. If you are new to running, start by getting comfortable with the distance rather than trying to beat the cut-off on every outing.

Confirm the length of your practice route. Four rounds of a neighbourhood ground are not necessarily four laps of a standard track. Your roll number slip or test instructions should determine when and where to report. For the Islamabad endurance test, advertisement coverage advises suitable shoes, drinking water and a medical check before participation.

Medical test requirements: what to verify

Passing the run establishes your endurance result; it does not establish medical fitness for appointment. For Islamabad Dolphin Force, the reported eyesight standard is 6/6 distance vision with or without glasses, and near vision no worse than J-1. If you wear glasses, that wording matters. Do not assume that glasses automatically disqualify you, or that every provincial service uses the Islamabad rule.

If you are unsure about eyesight, ask an eye professional to record both your near and distance vision and take the exact advertisement wording to the appointment. A private examination can help you understand your measurements; the authorized recruitment medical assessment determines the official result. Check your call letter for the designated facility, identification and any reports to bring before paying for private tests.

How to apply and follow the selection process

  1. Save the dated advertisement. Note the district, category, reference number and closing date. Check for an amendment issued for that same recruitment.
  2. Submit by the stated route. For online registration, keep the application reference, payment receipt and final confirmation. For a paper form, use the specified office and retain the acknowledgement where available.
  3. Prepare for the written test from the official syllabus. Match the force, rank and batch before using a past-papers PDF. A previous paper can help you practise timing but cannot confirm this year’s subjects, marks or pass threshold.
  4. Track each result separately. A physical-test pass list, interview shortlist, merit list and final selection list serve different purposes. Match your roll number and district, then read the notice for the next reporting date or document request.

Bottom line

Find the notice that matches your domicile and post. Write down its height standard, distance, time limit and closing date on one page. Use that page for practice, the official application channel for registration, and your own call letter for test-day instructions.
