What Is the Apni Chhat Apna Ghar Scheme 2026?

Apni Chhat Apna Ghar is a Punjab government housing programme for low-income families. For people who already own a plot but cannot afford to build, it offers interest-free loans. The portal now shows two financing options:

  1. Full Loan under the Apni Chhat, Apna Ghar programme
  2. Partial Loan under the Apni Chhat, Mehfooz Chhat programme, for roof repair or adding rooms or a floor

Under Mehfooz Chhat, the Punjab government says eligible homeowners can get up to Rs500,000 for repairing an unsafe roof and up to Rs1 million for an additional room or floor. Monthly instalments are reported at about Rs4,700 and Rs9,300 respectively, over nine years, and the government bears processing fees.

Do not confuse it with Apni Zameen Apna Ghar. That is a separate scheme for free plots with its own portal (azag.punjab.gov.pk).

Apni Chhat Apna Ghar Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must:

  • Be a permanent resident of Punjab with a valid CNIC
  • Own a residential property of up to 5 marla in urban areas or 10 marla in rural areas
  • Be 21 to 60 years old at the time of application
  • Have a clean financial record, with no loan defaults with banks or financial institutions
  • Have no conviction for a heinous criminal offence
  • Consent to verification of personal and property records through NADRA and PLRA

How to Apply for Apni Chhat Apna Ghar Scheme Online (Step by Step)

This is the registration process for the Apni Chhat Apna Ghar online application form:

  1. Visit the official portal: acag.punjab.gov.pk.
  2. Register an account (Apni Chhat Apna Ghar registration through online portal) with your personal details, then log in.
  3. Choose your loan category: Full Loan or Partial Loan (Mehfooz Chhat).
  4. Fill in personal and property information.
  5. Upload documents. This is how to upload documents for Apni Chhat Apna Ghar: select each file (CNIC, Fard, house photos) in the upload section and attach it.
  6. Submit and save your confirmation or tracking details.

You can also visit a facilitation centre. Help desks are set up at the offices of all Deputy Commissioners and Assistant Commissioners across Punjab, and at the offices of PHATA's regional and deputy directors.

Apni Chhat Apna Ghar Loan Application Requirements: Interest-Free Loan Documents

For the interest-free loan, the core file is the same: CNIC, Fard, and house photographs, plus the personal and property details entered in the form. For the amount you can borrow, the loan category you choose decides the limit.

What Is the Property Verification Process?

After you submit, the government verifies your records. This is the property verification process and document verification step:

  1. NADRA checks your identity and family record.
  2. PLRA checks that the Fard and land record match.
  3. Other eligibility checks, such as loan defaults, are applied.

Mismatched names, spellings, or plot details between your CNIC and Fard are the most likely cause of trouble. Fix such issues before applying.

How to Check Apni Chhat Apna Ghar Application Status

Log in to your account on the ACAG portal to track your application. The portal was built as a central platform to register, track, and manage applications. If you cannot see your status, call the toll-free helpline 0800-09100 or visit a facilitation centre.

What Is the Application Correction Process?

We did not find an official published procedure for editing a submitted application. If you entered something wrong, contact the helpline or the nearest facilitation centre and ask whether the portal allows a correction or whether you must reapply. Do not rely on agents.

What Are Common Reasons for Application Rejection?

The government has not published an official rejection list that we could find. Based on the stated eligibility rules, the likely reasons are:

  1. Age outside 21 to 60
  2. Plot larger than 5 marla (urban) or 10 marla (rural)
  3. Loan default on record
  4. CNIC and Fard details that do not match
  5. Not a permanent Punjab resident
  6. Missing or unclear documents or photos
  7. A heinous criminal conviction

Check each point before you submit.

How Does the Beneficiary Selection Process Work?

After verification, eligible applicants are shortlisted by the programme. The exact selection and balloting method is not described in the sources we reviewed, so follow official announcements on the portal. The scheme was introduced to help families who own land but cannot afford to build, with a first-phase target of 70,000 families receiving interest-free loans, and later reports describe over 166,000 loans disbursed by August 2026.
